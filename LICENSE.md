# Data licence & attribution

The data in this repository is derived from **Province of Manitoba open
data** (Manitoba GeoPortal / Manitoba Land Initiative), redistributed as
per-municipality historical GeoJSON snapshot shards (parcels, zoning,
development-plan designations) for the Manitoba parcel-search web app's
"as-of-year" view.

## Source

Dated provincial open-data snapshots (MBRoll GeoPackage parcels, Manitoba
Zoning By-Laws, Development Plan Designations) from the Manitoba GeoPortal
(geoportal.gov.mb.ca), Province of Manitoba. Shards are generated by
`r/build_historical_shards.R` in the
[manitoba-opendata-parcelsearch](https://github.com/jayschellenberg/manitoba-opendata-parcelsearch)
app repo. Each snapshot's `manifest.json` records its per-layer source dates.

## Licence

The underlying Province of Manitoba data is made available under the
**Open Government Licence – Manitoba**. Redistribution here is subject to
that licence. See the Manitoba GeoPortal / manitoba.ca open-data terms for
the authoritative licence text.
